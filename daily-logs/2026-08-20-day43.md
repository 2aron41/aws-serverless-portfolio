## What I Learned

An operational alarm is useful only if there is clear guidance for what the
signal means and what should happen next.

Different alarms represent different failure domains. A 4xx alarm should not
automatically be handled like a 5xx or Lambda failure. Its investigation
should first focus on client behavior, malformed traffic, request patterns,
and abuse signals.

A production runbook should also define when not to act. Explicit stop
conditions reduce the risk of turning an incident into a larger outage through
an unreviewed infrastructure change.
