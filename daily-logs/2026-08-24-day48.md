## What I Learned

- A failed command must invalidate any downstream count derived from its empty
  redirected output.
- A rollback mechanism and a rollback target are separate readiness concerns.
- Lambda aliases provide a stable routing point, but useful rollback requires
  retained known-good numbered versions.
- `$LATEST` should not be treated as an immutable recovery artifact.
- Active operational documentation must be updated as architecture evolves,
  while historical evidence should remain historically accurate.

## Next

Design a safe method for creating a legitimate second reviewed Lambda version
without manufacturing a fake rollback scenario.

Do not attempt a rollback rehearsal until a genuine older known-good numbered
version exists.
