## What I Learned

- Permission cleanup is safer when separated from traffic migration.
- Lambda aliases can have their own resource-based invoke permissions.
- An intentional Terraform destroy can be safe when the exact resource is
  reviewed and all unrelated resources are confirmed no-op.
- Terraform `count` can model mutually exclusive base-vs-alias permission
  behavior.
- A post-CI saved plan should be treated as immutable after review.
